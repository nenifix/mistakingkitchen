Our Story

About Mista King Kitchen

Born from a shared passion for authentic, soul-satisfying food.

Joseph & Godwin founded Mista King Kitchen with a simple belief: everyone deserves food that's prepared with care, quality ingredients, and bold, authentic flavors. What started as a dream between two friends has grown into a kitchen where every dish is crafted with intention — from the spice blend in our shawarma to the smoky depth of our Ghana Jollof Rice. We don't cut corners. We cook like royalty, and we serve it that way too.
